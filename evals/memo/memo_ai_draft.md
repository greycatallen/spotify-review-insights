# Spotify Product Insight: User Dissatisfaction & Core Experience Issues

## Recommendation
Prioritize resolving playback reliability and paywall friction. Playback stops/skips [C10-C12] represents the highest-severity issue (3.386010 [C11]), followed by premium feature lockout (2.905590 [C8]). Combined with overall dissatisfaction (12556 complaints [C1]), these issues directly undermine core listening experience and monetization perception.

## Evidence

Overall dissatisfaction dominates complaint volume at 12556 reviews [C1] with mean severity 2.058617 [C2]. The top technical issue—playback stops and skips unexpectedly—generated 3145 complaints [C10] at severity 3.386010 [C11], indicating users experience interruptions requiring app reinstalls when screens turn off.

Paywall friction ranks second by severity. Premium features lockout (replay, playback control) triggered 4025 complaints [C7] at severity 2.905590 [C8]. Core billing issues accumulated 1887 additional complaints [C16] at severity 2.722311 [C17], suggesting systematic frustration with feature paywalls and cancellation difficulty.

Ad frequency concerns affected 5084 free users [C4] at severity 2.322777 [C5]. Sync and feature limitations (missing device sync, no loop/podcast history) generated 3648 complaints [C13] at severity 2.589090 [C14]. Recent updates reportedly degraded quality for 2300 users [C19], and playlist shuffle issues affected 1621 users [C22] at severity 2.925355 [C23].

## Limits

Analysis covers 100000 verified reviews from 100013 in scope (0.6564 topic agreement, 0.6972 intent agreement). These are self-selected public reviews; complaint patterns reflect stated user experience, not verified account outcomes. Paywall mentions do not indicate actual subscription cancellations. Feature lockout frustration may reflect pricing strategy expectations rather than technical defects.
