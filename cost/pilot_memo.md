# Product Insight Memo: Critical App Quality Issues

## Summary & Recommendation

User reviews reveal widespread quality concerns demanding immediate attention. Overall app quality concerns lead with 11 complaints [C1] and mean severity 1.909091 [C2], followed by acute playback failures (4 complaints [C4], severity 3.500000 [C5]). We recommend prioritizing music playback interruptions and feature accessibility—both deliver high severity and directly impact core product value.

## Evidence

**Top Issues by Priority:**

1. **Overall app quality concerns**: 11 complaints [C1], mean severity 1.909091 [C2]. Users describe significant functionality decline versus competitors.

2. **Music playback interruptions**: 4 complaints [C4], mean severity 3.500000 [C5]—the highest severity metric. Includes mid-play stops, unwanted shuffling, and freezes.

3. **Premium paywall on basics**: 4 complaints [C7], mean severity 3.000000 [C8]. Song selection and skipping now gated behind premium.

4. **Recent update removed features**: 4 complaints [C10], mean severity 2.750000 [C11]. Users lost rewinding, repeat, and playhead controls.

5. **Audio bugs & ad overload**: 4 complaints [C13], mean severity 2.500000 [C14]. Audio adjustment failures combined with excessive ad interruptions.

6. **Ad frequency & volume**: 4 complaints [C16], mean severity 2.250000 [C17]. Disproportionately loud ads between songs.

7. **Post-update degradation**: 3 complaints [C19], mean severity 2.666667 [C20]. Users uninstalling following recent releases.

8. **Playlist navigation limits**: 3 complaints [C22], mean severity 2.333333 [C23]. Forced listening with restricted skip options.

## Limits

This analysis covers 100 self-selected public reviews with 0.8261 topic agreement and 0.913 intent agreement in verification sampling (n=23). Reviews reflect stated user experience, not verified account outcomes. No causality claims are made regarding retention or revenue impact.
