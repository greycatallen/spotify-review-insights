# Product Insight: Critical Playback and Engagement Issues Driving User Dissatisfaction

## Recommendation
Prioritize stabilizing playback reliability and addressing general app performance. Playback pauses and failures [C4–C6] warrant immediate investigation despite lower complaint volume than general dissatisfaction, given their elevated severity of 3.458333 [C5]. General app dissatisfaction [C1–C3] dominates complaint volume at 53 complaints [C1] with mean severity 1.981132 [C2]; root-cause analysis is needed to understand whether this stems from specific failures (playback, billing, ads) or broader quality perception.

## Evidence

**Top Issues by Priority Score:**
- General app dissatisfaction: 53 complaints [C1], mean severity 1.981132 [C2], priority score 105 [C3]
- Playback pauses and failures: 24 complaints [C4], mean severity 3.458333 [C5], priority score 83 [C6]
- Basic features locked behind paywall: 27 complaints [C7], mean severity 2.888889 [C8], priority score 78 [C9]
- Excessive and loud advertisements: 24 complaints [C10], mean severity 2.250000 [C11], priority score 54 [C12]
- Poor navigation and interface design: 15 complaints [C13], mean severity 2.533333 [C14], priority score 38 [C15]
- False connection errors despite internet: 11 complaints [C16], mean severity 3.363636 [C17], priority score 37 [C18]
- Limited song selection in free tier: 9 complaints [C19], mean severity 3.111111 [C20], priority score 28 [C21]
- Frustration with paid-feature model: 9 complaints [C22], mean severity 2.888889 [C23], priority score 26 [C24]

## Limits
Analysis covers 500 reviews (all completed; 0 quarantined). Verification sample of 65 reviews achieved 0.8462 topic and intent agreement. These are self-selected public reviews; numbers reflect complaint frequency and stated severity, not causation or user impact breadth.
