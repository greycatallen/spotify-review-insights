# Spotify Product Insight Memo

## Recommendation
Prioritize immediate fixes for core playback functionality and recent feature removals. The top two issues—Overall app quality concerns (11 complaints [C1], severity 1.91 [C2]) and Recent update removed core features (5 complaints [C4], severity 3.0 [C5])—represent the most critical user friction points. Address player controls (seek, repeat, song selection) restoration before addressing secondary concerns like ad frequency.

## Evidence Summary

User complaints center on functionality regression:

- **Overall app quality concerns** drove 11 complaints [C1] with mean severity 1.909091 [C2], yielding priority score 21 [C3].
- **Recent update removed core features** generated 5 complaints [C4] at severity 3.0 [C5] (priority 15 [C6])—the highest severity issue by impact.
- **Playback stops and skips songs** affected 4 users [C7] with severity 3.5 [C8] (priority 14 [C9]), indicating critical disruption to core listening.
- **Basic features locked behind premium** generated 4 complaints [C10] at severity 3.0 [C11] (priority 12 [C12]).
- **Multiple bugs and excessive ads** produced 4 complaints [C13] with severity 2.5 [C14] (priority 10 [C15]).
- **Excessive ad frequency** affected 4 users [C16] at severity 2.25 [C17] (priority 9 [C18]).
- **Forced full playlist playback with limited skips** involved 3 complaints [C19] with severity 2.67 [C20] (priority 8 [C21]).
- **Inconsistent lyrics display** generated 2 complaints [C22] at severity 2.5 [C23] (priority 5 [C24]).

## Limitations

This analysis covers 100 completed reviews with 22 verification samples (90.91% topic agreement, 95.45% intent agreement). These are self-selected public reviews; complaint counts reflect user feedback only and do not establish causation or quantify actual feature impact. Severity scores represent reviewer assessment, not normalized business impact metrics.
